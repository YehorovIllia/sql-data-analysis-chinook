Business Analysis: Revenue Optimization for Chinook Store
### 📊 Key Insights: Phase 1 (Market Analysis)

After analyzing the global sales data, I discovered several high-potential opportunities:

* **Market Leaders:** The **USA** and **Canada** remain our primary revenue drivers, accounting for over 50% of total sales.
* **Efficiency Leaders:** While the USA has the highest volume, **Chile**, **Ireland**, and **Hungary** show the highest **Average Check ($6.52 - $6.66)**. 
* **Strategic Recommendation:** I recommend increasing marketing efforts in the Chilean and Irish markets, as customers there demonstrate a higher willingness to spend per transaction compared to our top-volume markets.
